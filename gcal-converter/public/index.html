How does this work? (and is it safe?)

When you share a raw calendar.app.google link, Slack fetches Google's page and shows Google's generic preview — you can't change it. CalCard gives you a link on your own domain that serves your preview tags to Slack's crawler, then instantly forwards the actual person to your real booking page.

  • Nothing is stored. Your title, description and image are encoded into the link itself — there's no database and no account.
  • Your colleagues just use it. Anyone can open this page, paste their own link, and get their own branded version. No setup per person.
  • Only http(s) destinations are allowed, so the redirect can't be abused.
