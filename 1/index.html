From Daniel Ahmadizadeh
To B2B founders who’ve raised
Subject An experiment: 5 founders, 90 days

Hey,

I helped grow Crustdata from $600K to $14M ARR in 18 months. Now I’m running an experiment: five founders, only from YC, South Park Commons, Collide, and Orange Collective.

Keep reading if:

  • You have customers, but it doesn’t feel like PMF yet.
  • You’ve spent on GTM (or you’re about to), and meetings aren’t turning into momentum.
  • Your next round depends on a growth story you can’t tell yet.

If you’re pre-PMF, you’re pre-GTM. Most founders skip that step. They raise, then hire a seller, buy a tool, or sign an agency. Meetings happen, but nothing compounds.

I learned this the hard way. My YC company went from $5K to $85K a month during the batch, then shut down. I forgot about Barry Jenkins, our champion in Virginia Beach. Most founders don’t know who their Barry is.

Last month, a founder’s whole website said “manufacturing.” I asked who meaningfully loves what he’s built. He named an operator at a daycare. That’s his new experiment!

I’ll be your pre-GTM cofounder for 90 days:

  • Weeks 1–2: find your One.
  • Weeks 3–8: test it with new positioning, emails, and calls.
  • Weeks 9–12: get GTM-ready. You walk away with your One, real signal on whether people like them will commit time, money, or reputation, and the positioning, list of 100, and scripts your team can run.

Weekly 1:1s with me, no handoffs. If it helps, I’ll fly out to meet your One. Month to month, no equity.

My hope is that by month three, I’m asking to invest (if you’ll have me).

I’m taking five founders, and spots close November 30. Grab a time, and come ready for one question: who’s the one customer that meaningfully loves you, and what’s their name?

Daniel
